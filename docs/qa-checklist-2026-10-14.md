# Temperature Converter — QA Checklist

- Verify Celsius-to-Fahrenheit and Fahrenheit-to-Celsius conversions.
- Test the freezing point of water and absolute zero boundaries.
- Check Kelvin conversions and reject temperatures below 0 K.
- Handle blank and non-numeric input with clear feedback.
- Confirm conversion precision is consistent in both directions.
- Ensure changing the unit selector refreshes the displayed result.
